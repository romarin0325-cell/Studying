## Runtime preparation

The generator returned RGB checkerboard backgrounds for the original transparency requests. A targeted image-generation edit changed these to a chroma export. `render.js` keys those exports and suppresses edge spill at source resolution before downsampling to cached transparent 384px sprites. Stage panels are converted to JPEG for the downloadable build and cached individually in memory. No image work occurs on a remote service during play.
