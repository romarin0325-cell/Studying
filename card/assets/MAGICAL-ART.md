# Magical Girl assets

`magical-sanctuary.png` and `magical-card.png` were created with the built-in imagegen tool for DREAMWEAVER. They are decorative UI assets, never replacements for character portraits. Both are embedded by `build.mjs` so offline export needs no extra theme files.

Art direction: unapologetically pink magical-girl ribbons, winged hearts and spell seals; matte 2D shapes, restrained shading, no shiny metallic filigree. Text and controls are rendered by the game, not baked into art. The card back was visually checked at mobile card size.

## Background prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE production background illustration asset for a portrait mobile magical-girl card RPG, no UI mockup. 1024 by 1536 portrait. Theme: unabashedly cute, embarrassingly pink magical-girl transformation sanctuary. A blush pink and milk-white little magical boudoir stage in clouds, giant soft ribbon arch and winged heart brooch near the top, floating heart-shaped petals and small powder-pink magic circles at the outer edges, delicate lace silhouette in lower corners. Middle 65% is an airy almost-solid pale pink space so real game cards and text panels can sit above it. Hand-painted 2D Japanese shoujo game art direction, clean confident shapes, matte gouache-like flat colors, only two levels of soft shading, restrained dusty rose outlines. Strong charming identity, carefully composed, not a mass of ornaments. Absolutely no text, letters, people, logos, buttons, interface, watermarks. No photorealism, plastic, glossy 3D, metallic filigree, lens flare, sparkly noise or excessive detail. Output the actual background art edge to edge.

## Card-back prompt

Create one production card-back illustration for a mobile magical-girl card RPG. Vertical 3:5 proportion artwork, edge to edge, no mockup. Embarrassingly sweet pink shoujo transformation motif: one large flat heart-shaped pink brooch with tiny white wings and a floppy milk-white ribbon centered inside one delicate circular spell seal. Candy pink and cream, dusty rose outlines, a restrained scalloped border well inside the edges, small paired heart flourishes top and bottom. Strong confident simple shapes legible when displayed 80px wide. Matte hand-painted 2D commercial Japanese game illustration, just two levels of shading, no gradients of metallic shine, no plastic, no excessive jewelry, no sparkle noise, no text, no letters, no characters, no logos, no watermarks. Pretty and unabashedly magical-girly, simple balanced composition with generous empty pink space around the central emblem.
