## emerald_beetle

Pixel-art battle sprite strip for a side-view turn-based RPG. One wide image on a flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. Three poses of the SAME character in three equal-width cells, left to right: (1) idle battle stance, (2) attack with the main weapon or spell mid-motion, (3) hurt, recoiling backwards with eyes shut. The character faces LEFT in all three poses. Character: an emerald green jewel-shelled beetle with ornate gold bands, armor and green legs, matching the first reference. Match the second and third reference images (the style anchors) exactly in: size of one art pixel, outline thickness, number of shading steps, head-to-body ratio and how much of the cell the idle pose fills. Keep the creature's own proportions from the reference. All three poses have the same head size and stand on the same feet baseline. The attack pose keeps the head at nearly the same height as idle and leans forward no more than 15 degrees; the weapon may extend at most half a cell. True pixel art: large uniform square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no glow, no soft shading, no drop shadow, no ground line. One-pixel outline in a single dark navy-purple colour (#2b2140) around the whole silhouette; the outline touches the pure magenta background directly, with no pink or magenta pixels on or inside the character. Three-step cel shading per colour area, light from the upper left. At most 24 colours in total. No text, no borders, no UI, no second character. Small sparks are allowed only inside the attack cell.
