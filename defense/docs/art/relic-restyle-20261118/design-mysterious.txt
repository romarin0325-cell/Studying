DESIGN NOTES FOR THIS SHEET. Keep the rendering exactly as in the style rules above and match the attached sheet of relics from this same set.
- The watch and the crystal must feel MYSTERIOUS and quietly magical, NOT cute: no faces, no nightcaps, no sleeping moons, no decorative bows, no chubby toy proportions. Use a deep midnight-violet and navy palette with cool silver-blue accents; elegant, slender, slightly asymmetric and sharp shapes. Mystery comes from what is seen inside the glass or gem, painted as one or two quiet flat cel shapes in a paler tone (a frozen crescent moon, a few faint stars), never from glow halos, sparkles, particles or galaxy textures.
- The two music-note icons stay friendly and simple like the rest of the common set.
