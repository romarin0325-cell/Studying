DESIGN DENSITY (this is a COMMON / RARE sheet). Keep the rendering exactly as in the style rules above. These icons sit one step BELOW the attached high-rarity relics in richness: a Common (C) relic is a main object plus 1-2 small secondary parts in 2 materials with one small hand-drawn detail (a stitch, a knot, a painted dot, a tiny leaf); a Rare (R) relic is a main object plus 2-3 secondary parts in 2-3 materials. Like the character sprites, detail comes from parts and small motifs drawn with the same thin brown line and flat cel colours, never from shine. Silhouettes should be pleasant and slightly asymmetric. Motifs come from this game's world: a star garden at night with roses, hydrangeas, moons, stars, ribbons and small rabbits. No halo, no backplate, no sparkles around objects.
REFERENCES (extra): the attached sheet of richer relics shows the high-rarity tier of this same set; match their line, shading and palette exactly, but keep these C/R objects simpler than them.
