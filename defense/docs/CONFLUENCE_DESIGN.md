# ASTRA — 성좌의 수호자

## Product contract

A portrait, offline, single-HTML merge defense. The player chooses six of the existing 21 characters; only those six can be summoned. All 25 cells are available immediately. Every character attacks, including economy and support characters. A normal expedition contains 12 waves, three bosses, and three visits to a merchant; the victory screen can continue into an endless siege. No permanent attack multiplier or mandatory grind precedes the fun.

The opening is playable immediately: two Zekes and a support hero are already firing, currency permits several summons, and the first merge is available before any explanatory panel. A short contextual hint describes only the next useful action. The player's first ultimate is available during the opening wave. The first boss arrives at wave four.

## Decisions, with visible consequences

- Summon costs rise during the run. A shuffled six-character bag limits unplayable streaks while retaining uncertainty. Summons never draw outside the chosen deck.
- Equal characters of equal rank merge into that character at the next rank. Rumi can serve as a same-rank wildcard. Merge damage scaling exceeds the two ingredients, but losing coverage, support links, separate economy units and early income creates a reason to delay.
- Moving and swapping are free. Supports affect orthogonally adjacent allies, making the 5×5 formation meaningful. Empty cells have identical neutral markings.
- Interest pays after a wave: 10% of unspent gold, capped at eight. The next payout is always visible. Queen earns dividends while occupying board space; Cinderella returns an immediate burst of money when consumed in a merge. All income sources appear separately in the wave settlement.
- All six deck skills spend one shared star gauge. Every skill requires a deployed copy. Choosing burst, freeze, haste, gravity or income has an opportunity cost. Skill strength uses the strongest deployed copy, never the sum of duplicate copies.
- A merchant after waves 3, 6 and 9 offers deterministic hero purchases and build-changing artifacts. A reroll has a visible price. Skipping preserves savings. Boss victories award one of three artifacts for free.
- Bosses announce a concrete action before it happens. Formation relocation, saving a control skill and focusing damage can answer that action.

## Presentation contract

The game is a painted fantasy battlefield with a compact frame, not a dashboard of stat cards. The arena occupies most of the phone. The six skill portraits and summon control stay under the thumb. Rank pips, a thin enemy health bar, merge destinations and the boss warning are the only persistent combat overlays. Detailed numbers appear on selection.

The author rejected the initial four-pose approach because the poses did not form continuous motion. Do not ship those candidate sheets or interpret separate illustrations as animation. The current direction is four authored cardinal views of one compact approximately two-head SD piece per character: down/front, up/back, left and right. The same neutral posture and anatomical head scale are preserved in all four views. The engine chooses the view toward the enemy; a short procedural anticipation/recoil indicates activity. Projectile direction, a clear travel path, synchronized impact and distinct elemental effects communicate the attack. Merge uses a brief inward pull and an outward star pulse. Ultimates use attack-specific effects and a short musical accent. Ordinary attacks never compete visually with an ultimate. Reduced effects removes shake, flashes and excess particles without hiding gameplay warnings.

Character identity comes from the user's originals in Downloads/캐릭터 and their concept memo. Costume, gender and face are invariants. For the two-head sprites, head size and face recognition take priority over height variation (the author's latest instruction). Preserve the supplied tall/medium/short categories as source metadata for future use, but do not enlarge or shrink an entire character to represent height. Accessories do not determine body scale. Assets are reviewed at actual phone size and on both light and dark backgrounds. The newly supplied 은토끼.png supersedes the old white-coat rabbit design.

## Delivery and evidence

The existing Defense remains in Git history. The active index and offline release open this game. Release gates cover deck confinement, merging, income, shared gauge, boss interactions, save/resume, offline execution, touch layout and failed image loads. Human-readable play notes distinguish observed behavior from judgments about fun. Desktop emulation cannot certify physical Android performance.
