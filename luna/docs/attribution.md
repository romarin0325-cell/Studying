# Artwork and audio

- `assets/portrait.webp`: optimized derivative of the original Luna illustration
  supplied by the user for this game. Its distinctive lavender bob, violet eyes,
  black lace hood, violet jewel, and gold accents are preserved.
- `assets/thinking.webp`, `assets/warm.webp`, `assets/resolve.webp`: technical
  crops and WebP exports from the Luna expression sheet generated specifically
  for this project from the user's authorized reference. These are used in story
  scenes and endings, rather than replacing the supplied protagonist design.
- The in-combat Luna figure, enemies, arenas, particles, and attack telegraphs
  are original procedural Canvas drawings. They do not use third-party sprites.
- Music and effects are synthesized in Web Audio from original note patterns
  written for this game. There are no downloaded recordings or external fonts.

The user authorized these assets in this game and its pull request. That does
not imply permission to redistribute the underlying character artwork elsewhere
or any claim about its original ownership. No third-party license is invented.
